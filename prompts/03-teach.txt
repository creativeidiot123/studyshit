Build the topic exactly as LEARNING FORMAT and INTERACTIVE PARTS in /prompts/00-shared.txt describe: title, hook, 1-4 concept blocks (plain idea with inline one-line definitions, formula with symbols, step-by-step example, visual where useful, instant-feedback quick check), optional "Try it" calculator, optional Beyond the slides box, Remember bullets, "Got it" checkbox. Write for a student who missed the lecture and wants to get it fast:
- lead with the hook and the intuition; bring in the formula only once the idea is clear;
- show, then let them try: an example, a picture and a question beat another paragraph;
- redraw an exam-relevant slide chart or table as a clean SVG or HTML table rather than describing it;
- define only terms the lecture uses, once, inline; never a list of definitions;
- keep all lecturer numbers, notation, formulas, warnings and source IDs (data-source-ids); when condensing an older long topic, cut explanation, never source facts;
- no formal derivations, proofs or limitation essays unless the lecturer covered them; a short "why it works" intuition is fine;
- add one "Beyond the slides" box only if an important closely related idea is missing (see BEYOND THE SLIDES); no external research yet.
Prose 200-450 words, hard cap 550 (see LIMITS for what counts). Recalculate every number in examples, visuals, quick checks and the calculator; a "Try it" calculator must reproduce the lecturer's example result at its default inputs.
