L013 · Slide 13 · Trading

Primary market: selling newly issued shares to investors (IPO or secondary offering in lecturer terminology). Secondary market: existing previously issued shares. Common stock: ownership shares of publicly held corporation. Flag terminology: secondary offering can also mean resale of existing shares; distinguish actual issuance.

Visual annotation targets: Three labels primary, secondary, common stock.

L016 · Slide 16 · Funds

Exchange-traded funds (ETFs): portfolios of stocks bought/sold in one trade; lecturer emphasizes passive index tracking; providers BlackRock, Vanguard, State Street. SPDRs (Standard & Poor’s Depository Receipts, spiders) track several S&P indexes. Closed-end mutual funds also invest in securities portfolios; lecturer says foremost actively managed. Flag passive wording as generalization rather than universal definition.

Visual annotation targets: ETF, SPDR and closed-end funds bullet groups.

L017 · Slide 17 · Financial indicators

Book value: balance-sheet net worth dependent on accounting standards (IFRS, US-GAPP as printed); slide links to liquidation value, but do not treat them as identical. Dividend: periodic cash distribution to shareholders. P/E = price per share / earnings per share (ratio). Market-value balance sheet uses market values for assets/liabilities.

Visual annotation targets: Four terms; book-value caveat.
